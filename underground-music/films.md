# Films

## Films we watched

* ***American Hardcore*** (2006), directed by Paul Rachman and written by Steven Blush, based on Blush's book. Topic [3](/underground-music/topic-03.html).
* ***The Shield Around the K*** (2000), directed by Heather Rose Dominic, about K Records. Topic [5](/underground-music/topic-05.html), with K Records in Topic [4](/underground-music/topic-04.html).
* ***Instrument*** (1999), directed by Jem Cohen, about Fugazi. Topics [6](/underground-music/topic-06.html), [8](/underground-music/topic-08.html) and [14](/underground-music/topic-14.html).
* ***Metal: A Headbanger's Journey*** (2005), directed by Sam Dunn, Scot McFadyen and Jessica Joy Wise. Topic [7](/underground-music/topic-07.html).
* ***Spend an Evening with Saddle Creek*** (2005), directed by Rob Walters and Jason Kulbel, about the first ten years of Saddle Creek Records. Topic [10](/underground-music/topic-10.html).
* ***Danielson: A Family Movie*** (2006), directed by J.L. Aronson. Topic [13](/underground-music/topic-13.html).

## Also mentioned

* ***1991: The Year Punk Broke*** (1992), directed by Dave Markey, a documentary of Sonic Youth's tour with Nirvana and other bands. Topic [2](/underground-music/topic-02.html).
* ***Lucky Three***, Jem Cohen's short film about Elliott Smith. Topic [14](/underground-music/topic-14.html).
* A clip from ***Dogtown*** (chapters 20 and 21) on skateboard culture. Topic [3](/underground-music/topic-03.html).
