# Texts and Readings

The course used five main texts, though not every text was used every semester. The links below go to WorldCat, a library catalog that shows which libraries near you hold a copy.

## The five main texts

* Azerrad, Michael. *Our Band Could Be Your Life: Scenes from the American Indie Underground 1981-1991.* Back Bay Books, 2001. [Find it in a library](https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/0316787531). 
	* Used in Topics [1](/underground-music/topic-01.html), [2](/underground-music/topic-02.html), [5](/underground-music/topic-05.html), [6](/underground-music/topic-06.html) and [8](/underground-music/topic-08.html).
* Greenberg, Arielle. *Youth Subcultures: Exploring Underground America.* Longman, 2007. [Find it in a library](https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/0321241940). 
	* Used in Topics [1](/underground-music/topic-01.html), [7](/underground-music/topic-07.html), [8](/underground-music/topic-08.html), [9](/underground-music/topic-09.html), [11](/underground-music/topic-11.html) and [12](/underground-music/topic-12.html).
* Greenwald, Andy. *Nothing Feels Good: Punk Rock, Teenagers, and Emo.* St. Martin's Griffin, 2003. [Find it in a library](https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/0312308639). 
	* Used in Topics [8](/underground-music/topic-08.html), [9](/underground-music/topic-09.html), [10](/underground-music/topic-10.html) and [11](/underground-music/topic-11.html).
* Kruse, Holly. *Site and Sound: Understanding Independent Music Scenes.* [Find it in a library](https://www.worldcat.org/search?q=Site+and+Sound+Understanding+Independent+Music+Scenes+Kruse). 
	* Used in Topics [1](/underground-music/topic-01.html), [4](/underground-music/topic-04.html) and [6](/underground-music/topic-06.html).
* Sinker, Daniel, ed. *We Owe You Nothing: Punk Planet: The Collected Interviews.* Expanded edition. Punk Planet Books/Akashic Books, 2008. [Find it in a library](https://www.worldcat.org/isbn/9781933354323). 
	* Used in Topics [1](/underground-music/topic-01.html), [2](/underground-music/topic-02.html), [4](/underground-music/topic-04.html), [5](/underground-music/topic-05.html), [6](/underground-music/topic-06.html), [8](/underground-music/topic-08.html), [9](/underground-music/topic-09.html), [11](/underground-music/topic-11.html), [12](/underground-music/topic-12.html) and [14](/underground-music/topic-14.html).
