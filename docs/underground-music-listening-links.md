## How these were found

For each of the 78 songs on `underground-music-listening.md`, I ran a web search limited to youtube.com and picked the result that looked like the plain studio recording (usually an upload titled just the song name, or a label-provided audio upload) over live, cover, lyric and full-album videos. **I could not play any of them**, so I only know the title and description the search showed. Each one needs a listen before it goes on the site. Where a video id is shown as an alternate, it is a second choice (for example the official video, or a remaster). To use an alternate, the link is `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=` followed by the id.

Confidence column:
- **likely**: the title and artist match, and the upload looks like the studio recording.
- **CHECK**: probably right, but something doesn't line up with our list (album, spelling, or which version).
- **WEAK**: a fan upload, a live recording, or a whole-album video. Better to find another.
- **NOT FOUND**: no usable result. Needs another search, or Micah may know the song under a different title.

Tally (before Micah's answers): 49 likely, 21 CHECK, 5 WEAK, 3 NOT FOUND. Since then: #49 has a link from Micah and #69 now has a candidate; Micah has also supplied links for #43 and #58, so nothing is missing a link.
